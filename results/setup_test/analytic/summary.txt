    Nose and inlet Mach: 1.6

1. Panel method (Hess-Smith) on the wing's own section
   lift slope 6.502 per rad against 2 pi = 6.283 from thin-airfoil theory (+3.5 %, the thickness effect)
   at 3 deg: cl 0.3909, cm about the quarter chord -0.0111

2. Prandtl lifting line (Glauert series) on the main wing planform
   aspect ratio 3.14, CL_alpha 3.888 per rad (Helmbold-style finite-wing value), span efficiency 0.979
   at 6 deg: CL 0.3573, induced drag CDi 0.01322

3. Sears-Haack area rule
   volume 20.09 m3, length 15.16 m: ideal body D/q = 128 V^2/(pi L^4) = 0.3114 m2 (the code's slender-body sum gives 0.3157)
   the design's area distribution: D/q 0.6342 m2 = 2.04 x the ideal

4. Nose cone and inlet at Mach 1.6
   nose half-angle 15.0 deg (Taylor-Maccoll): shock 42.0 deg, surface Cp 0.2288, surface Mach 1.361
   pitot inlet normal shock: M2 0.668, p2/p1 2.820, T2/T1 1.388, total-pressure recovery 0.8952

5. Ackeret supersonic section theory at Mach 1.8 (normal Mach 1.509 across the 33 deg sweep)
