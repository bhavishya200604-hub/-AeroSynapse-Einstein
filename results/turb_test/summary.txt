Turbulence and flow instability
===============================

Setup:
  Source:
    3D flow results: results/cfd3d/live_gust folder
  Flight condition:
    Mach number: 0.7
    Altitude: 3000 m
    Angle of attack: 1 deg
  Transition:
    Free-stream Tu: 0.1 %
    Critical N (override): 0
    Section station: 0.35 of semi-span
    Surface: smooth
  Wake instability:
    Wake stations: 0.15, 0.4, 0.8 x / L behind the tail
    Collocation points: 80
    Largest wavenumber: 3 1 / wake half-width
  Spectra:
    Spectrum segments: 4

1. Free-stream turbulence and transition on the wing section
   section of 'wing' at 35 % semi-span, chord 3.33 m, Re 4.11e+07, alpha 1 deg
   critical N 8.15 (Mack, Tu 0.1 %, surface smooth)
   upper surface: transition at x/c 0.0362, natural (Tollmien-Schlichting waves reach N crit)
   lower surface: transition at x/c 0.0984, natural (Tollmien-Schlichting waves reach N crit)
   section friction drag 0.00404 with this transition, 0.00444 fully turbulent (-9.0 %)
   Tu 0.01 % moves upper transition to x/c 0.0712; Tu 5.0 % to 0.0054
   limits: incompressible Thwaites layer on the panel-method velocity (Prandtl-Glauert Mach capped at 0.6 in the panel solution); no crossflow instability of the swept wing

2. Wake instability (inviscid Rayleigh equation, temporal, Chebyshev collocation)
   solver check, tanh mixing layer: maximum growth 0.1884 at k 0.434 (Michalke 1964: 0.1897 at 0.4446)
   x/L 0.15 (3D flow): deficit 0.84, half-width 0.693 m; sinuous mode grows at 0.131 U/b (e-folding 23.10 ms), 26.2 Hz, St_b 0.079; varicose 0.040 U/b
   x/L 0.4 (3D flow): deficit 0.59, half-width 0.733 m; sinuous mode grows at 0.091 U/b (e-folding 34.88 ms), 28.8 Hz, St_b 0.092; varicose 0.028 U/b
   x/L 0.8 (3D flow): deficit 0.31, half-width 1.065 m; sinuous mode grows at 0.048 U/b (e-folding 97.17 ms), 22.9 Hz, St_b 0.106; varicose 0.015 U/b
   the sinuous mode is the one that rolls the wake into a vortex street; the Gaussian fit smooths the coarse-grid profile

     left tip wake    Tu   3.46 %  integral length 1.94 m  no resolved peak (record too short: lowest frequency 55 Hz; run more steps)

Limits: the 3D flow is a coarse large-eddy simulation, so only eddies several cells across are resolved; the stability analysis is linear, inviscid and parallel-flow (no absolute/convective distinction).
